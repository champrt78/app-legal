Murphy's Files Uploader

Murphy's Files Uploader is a small private tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (Murphy's Files) and TikTok account. It is registered with TikTok under the name "Godzilla Uploader".

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos and Shorts the owner has made, with their titles, descriptions and schedule, to the owner's own accounts.
  • Uses Google's YouTube Data API (video upload only) and TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-in on this page.

Your data

The tool accesses only the accounts the owner connects. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties. Murphy's Files Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
