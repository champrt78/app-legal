Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Murphy's Files Uploader (registered with TikTok as "Godzilla Uploader") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.

What it accesses: basic profile info (account ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload or post videos, only for accounts the owner connects.

What it stores: access tokens, kept locally on the owner's computer and used only to upload videos. No data is sold, shared, or sent to third parties.

Removal: revoke access any time in your TikTok or Google account settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
